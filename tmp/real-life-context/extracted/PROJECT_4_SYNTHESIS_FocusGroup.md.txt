## 🎯 THE THREE CORE QUESTIONS TESTED

### Q1: Does this feel like a BeReal feature?
**Result:** ✅ YES (5/5 consensus)
- Aisha: "Completely."
- Tomás: "Same camera, same spirit."
- Sebastián: "Evolution of BeReal, not a copy. 'Live a moment with me' vs 'look my moment.'"
- Valentina: "Doesn't feel like fitness or Instagram. Feels like its own thing."
- Miriam: "Felt authentic."

### Q2: Does the iMessage invite work as a standalone pitch?
**Result:** ✅ YES, with refinement needed
- Valentina (non-user): "If my close friend sends it, yes. Not about the app, about doing something together."
- Valentina suggests more explicit copy: "Just you and me" — to make exclusivity clearer
- Miriam (non-user): "I'd download for my friend in Lagos immediately."
- Aisha: Full acceptance — wanted to run multiple challenges simultaneously

### Q3: Does "no streaks, no scores" create trust?
**Result:** ✅ YES, critical trust mechanism
- Tomás (Duolingo trauma): "No streaks changed everything. Lost a 200-day streak and never opened the app again. This is why I'd try Real-Life Challenges."
- Without the anti-streak framing, Tomás would say "no directly"
- The messaging is not a tagline — it's the primary trust signal

## 🚨 FRICTION POINTS IDENTIFIED

### 1. The Missing "Missed Day" Screen (CRITICAL)
**Mentioned independently by 2 participants:**
- Tomás: "Most important screen. What happens if I fail one day? That screen should be there."
- Sebastián: "If it feels like Duolingo when you lose, I never open the app again."

**Why this matters:** Day 2 feels easy. Day 20 (when tired) is when users know if the app is "kind or not."

**Participant suggestion (Valentina):** Allow the friend to send something human like "tomorrow we continue."

### 2. The One-Year Duration Option
Tomás: "The one year option is scary. Imagine failing a one-year challenge with your friend. No no no."
- The option exists visually and creates implied pressure
- Recommendation: Cap default or de-emphasize the year option

### 3. Copy Refinement Needed
Miriam (UK): "You up?" has a different connotation in British English (booty call)
- Not a blocker (friend name mitigates), but suggests copy needs A/B testing

### 4. Accessibility for Age 35+
Miriam: "Bigger text or light version. The dark screen with small letters — some people my age would struggle."
- Survey gave ease-of-use score of 3/5 (vs 4-5 from others)
- Recommendation: Test visual accessibility with older users

### 5. No Negotiation Step (Discovery)
Sebastián: "Let the receiver negotiate. Propose 5 days instead of 10, or change the action. Right now sender decides everything alone."
- Adds consent layer
- Increases adoption by giving agency to receiver
