A SMALL CHALLENGE, NOT A TRACKER.
Real-Life
Challenges.Small real-life challenges you make with people close to you. One
little thing, done together, captured the way BeReal already captures
life — honestly, and only once.
§ 01Feature context
BeReal captures one spontaneous moment a day. It does that beautifully. What it
doesn't quite do yet is help two people show up for something together beyond
watching each other post.
Real-Life Challenges lets you make a tiny challenge with someone close — drink
water today, take a walk, send a voice note, touch grass — and uses BeReal's
existing capture moment as the proof. The challenge becomes the reason to open
the app, and the reason to invite someone in.
The bigger idea: instead of "download BeReal to see my posts," the invitation
becomes "do this with me." A much stronger reason to install — especially for
users who don't yet have friends on the app and feel the empty-room problem.

§ 05State variations
VARIATION A
First-time vs Repeat
ENTRY First-time: opens via tab → sees empty card.
Repeat: opens via widget tap → drops in mid-flow.
ONBOARDING First-time: shows Screen 04 bridge card.
Repeat: skipped entirely.
COPY First-time: "No challenge yet. Start something small."
Repeat: "Maya drank her water 
 You up?"
RECAP First-time: empty, with one prompt card.
Repeat: previous days visible as small saved moments.
VARIATION B
Success vs Error
TRIGGER Success: post completes, friend's reveal unlocks.
Error: invite, post, or update fails to send/load.
UI Success: yellow banner + saved-to-recap motion.
Error: inline card with two buttons. No modal blocker.
COPY Success: "Nice. Day 1 is done. Saved to your recap."
Error: "That didn't go through. Want to try again?"
ACTIONS Success: Recap · See Maya's.
Error: Retry · Not now.
