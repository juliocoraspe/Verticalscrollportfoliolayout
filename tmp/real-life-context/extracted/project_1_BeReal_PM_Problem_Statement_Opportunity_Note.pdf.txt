BeReal PM · PROBLEM STATEMENT & OPPORTUNITY NOTE
PRODUCT
BeReal
FEATURE AREA / FOCUS
Core Posting Flow, Millennial (28+) Retention
01 PROBLEM
Millennial users (28+) experience cognitive
overload and mechanic opacity in BeReal's core
flow, leading to disengagement and churn in the
platform's most commercially underserved
demographic.
02 EVIDENCE / INSIGHTS
A Journey Map (Stages 1–3): "cognitive overload," "mild anxiety," and opaque dual-
camera + post-to-unlock mechanics across every stage. ⾭ USER JOURNEY MAP
B 28+ = only 8% of US MAU, yet millennials represent ~38% of all social media users
globally, a structural gap in BeReal's audience. ⾭ PEW RESEARCH, SOCIAL MEDIA BY AGE
C Downloads: 93.5M (2022) → ~6M est. (2025). Millennials show lower platform loyalty
than Gen Z, and cross-platform studies confirm this cohort's retention is the hardest to
sustain. ⾭ BUSINESSOFAPPS.COM, BEREAL STATISTICS
03 OPPORTUNITY
If BeReal adds subtle contextual cues at friction-
heavy moments in the posting flow, without altering
the authenticity mechanic, millennial users may feel
more oriented and be more likely to build consistent
habits.
04 POTENTIAL IMPACT
Retaining millennials expands BeReal's audience diversity, directly supporting RealBrands' appeal to premium
advertisers beyond Gen Z only campaigns.
