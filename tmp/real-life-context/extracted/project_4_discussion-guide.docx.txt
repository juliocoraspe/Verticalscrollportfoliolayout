[Normal] BeReal Feature Focus Group — Discussion Guide
[Normal] Feature: Real-Life Challenges
[Normal] Duration: 40 minutes
[Normal] Facilitator: Julio Coraspe
[Normal] Date: [Insert date]
[Normal] Warm-Up (5 mins)
[List Paragraph] How often do you use BeReal, and what makes you actually open the app on a given day?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Is there a day where you skip posting? What usually happens?
[List Paragraph] When you think about social apps in general, is there one that feels like it actually fits your life — not just another thing that asks for your attention?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: What makes it feel that way?
[Normal] Interest (5–7 mins)
[List Paragraph] When you first saw this feature, what did you think it was for?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Did it feel like something that belongs in BeReal, or did it remind you of another app?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: What specifically gave you that impression?
[List Paragraph] Imagine a close friend sent you a message saying: 'I invited you to a Real-Life Challenge — accept it and start Day 1.' Would you accept it?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Would your answer change if you did not have BeReal installed yet?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: What would make you more likely to say yes — or no?
[List Paragraph] Looking at the five challenge families — Movement, Mood, Care, Connection, Outside — did any of them feel like something you would actually want to do with someone?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Was there a family or a specific action that felt like it was missing?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Which one felt most natural to your life right now?
[Normal] Usability (10–12 mins)
[List Paragraph] On the screen where you set up the challenge — picking the action, the person, the rhythm, and the proof type — how did that feel?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Did it feel like too many decisions, or was it easy to move through?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Did it remind you at any point of a fitness app or a productivity tracker? Why?
[List Paragraph] When you saw the onboarding card after accepting the challenge — the one that says 'You're in. Maya too' — did it make you feel ready to start, or did you still have questions?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Were the three steps clear enough, or did you feel like you needed more explanation?
[List Paragraph] After you posted your proof and saw the screen that says 'Day 1 done, two more to go' — what did you feel?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Did that progress indicator feel motivating, or did it start to feel like an obligation?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Would you feel bad if you missed Day 2?
[Normal] Value & Impact (10–12 mins)
[List Paragraph] The next day, you get a notification that says: 'Maya drank her water. You up?' How does that make you feel?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Does it feel like a message from a friend, or does it feel like the app is pressuring you?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Would that kind of message make you open the app, or would you ignore it?
[List Paragraph] Do you think this feature fits with what BeReal is supposed to be — or does it start to feel like a different kind of app?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Is there anything about it that feels authentic? Is there anything that feels forced?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: If BeReal launched this, would it change how you think about the app?
[List Paragraph] Is there a specific person in your life — a friend, partner, sibling — that you would actually start a challenge with?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: What challenge would you choose with them?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: What would make you stop doing the challenge before it ends?
[List Paragraph] Thinking about the whole experience — from setting up the challenge to posting Day 1 to getting a notification on Day 2 — does this feel like something that would make you use BeReal more?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: What is the main thing that would make you keep coming back?
[Normal] Wrap-Up (3–5 mins)
[List Paragraph] If you could change one thing about this feature — just one — what would it be?
[List Paragraph] Would you want BeReal to actually launch this?
[List Paragraph] Follow-up: Would you tell someone about it?

[Normal] Share your short survey link in the chat before participants leave.

[List Paragraph] Note any repeated confusion, hesitation, or excitement.
