[Heading 1] Real-Life Challenges
[Heading 2] Problem
[Normal] BeReal captures one daily moment, but users need more reasons to open the app and share small real-life actions with close friends. Without a lightweight way to create shared wellness rituals, users turn to external apps for fitness, habit tracking, or accountability—fragmenting their social connection.
[Heading 2] Target User
[Normal] Gen Z and young millennial BeReal users who value authenticity, close-friend connection, and wellness without performance pressure. They want simple daily routines and accountability, not a fitness app or leaderboard.
[Heading 2] MAU Success Metrics
[Normal] Primary: % of monthly active users who start and complete at least one Real-Life Challenge (15–25% target). Secondary: weekly active participation rate, average challenge completion rate per user, and weekly return frequency.
[Heading 2] Assumptions & Non-Goals
[Normal] Assumptions: Users want more reasons to return monthly. Close-friend accountability makes small actions more fun and meaningful. Lightweight proof (photos, voice notes, check-ins) feels native to BeReal. Feature must feel playful, not optimised.  Non-Goals: Not a fitness or productivity app. No public leaderboards. No sensitive health data by default. No beauty filters or curated feeds. Not a mental health treatment tool.
[Heading 2] User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

TABLE 1
User Story | Acceptance Criteria
Create a challenge with a close friend or small group. | • Can initiate with one friend or group • Can choose action & rhythm before invite • Challenge starts when invite accepted
Choose a personal challenge type (not fitness-only). | • Types: movement, mood, care, connection, outside • Clear language explanations included • No fitness/health data required to join
Receive playful personal reminders. | • Includes context or friend’s name • Casual, human tone (not formal) • Can skip/ignore without public penalty
See lightweight updates from close friends. | • Quick access without full feed navigation • Shows progress, not public rankings • Can complete challenge or react simply
View challenge recap after completion. | • Access short recap when challenge ends • Shows shared progress & posted moments • Minimal, emotional tone (not dashboard)
